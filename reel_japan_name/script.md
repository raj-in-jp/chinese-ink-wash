kilometres to get here.          [JAPAN slammed over the map, slashed, 日本 stamped, WHO NAMED IT?, the route snakes round the world]

## Fact notes

- 日本 (Nihon / Nippon) = "sun" + "origin": the land in the direction of sunrise, seen from China; the name has been
  used since about the 7th century.
- Older Chinese pronunciations of 日本国 sound roughly like "Jih-pen-kuo"; Marco Polo's "Cipangu / Zipangu" (in his book
  of about 1300) is usually traced to it.  He described a land of gold palaces; he never went to Japan.
- Columbus (1492) sailed west hoping to reach Asia and Cipangu; he believed Cuba might be Cipangu.
- Malay "Jepang" (from southern Chinese) → Portuguese "Japão" (1500s) → English "Japan" (first spelled "Giapan" in 1577).
- The routes on the map are drawn simply: the Silk Road by land, the Portuguese sea route round Africa.
