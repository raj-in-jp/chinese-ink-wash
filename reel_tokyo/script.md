## Fact notes

- Nara (Heijō-kyō) became the capital in 710; Heian-kyō (Kyoto) in 794.  京都 = "capital" + "city (metropolis)".
- Edo: a castle town (Edo Castle, 1457) when Tokugawa Ieyasu took it over in 1590; seat of the shogunate from 1603.
  Around 1700 it had roughly a million people, among the largest cities in the world.  江戸 is usually read as
  "inlet / bay" + "door / entrance".
- 1868: Edo renamed Tokyo (東京, "eastern capital"); the emperor moved there in 1868–69.
- There is no law that explicitly designates Tokyo as Japan's capital; it is the capital by custom and practice.
- "160 years ago" counts from 2026 back to 1866, just before the renaming.
