## Fact notes (checked before recording)

- Jōmon pottery: among the oldest known in the world (c. 14,000 BC and earlier). Era start dates are conventional and
  scholars differ, especially for Jōmon/Yayoi; the reel shows rounded dates with "c.".
- Sengoku: 1467 (Ōnin War) to unification. It overlaps the late Muromachi and the Azuchi–Momoyama periods; the reel
  uses the familiar name Sengoku instead of Azuchi–Momoyama to keep the line short.
- Nara Daibutsu: about 15 m. Black ships: Commodore Perry, 1853.
- Great Kantō earthquake: 1923, in the Taishō era.
- Reiwa: taken from the Man'yōshū, the first era name drawn from a Japanese classic instead of a Chinese one.
- WWII is stated plainly and neutrally ("war, defeat in 1945"): no imagery of violence.
